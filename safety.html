Human Review & Safety

ProsightAI provides physiological information and context to support people. It does not replace human judgment.

01

Human Review

Outputs are advisory context. Interpretation and decisions remain with the person responsible for the workflow.

02

Safety Boundaries

ProsightAI does not diagnose medical conditions, recommend treatment, determine intent or threat, make employment or fitness decisions, or verify identity.

03

Privacy & Data Handling

ProsightAI is designed to minimize unnecessary data exposure. Storage and retention settings depend on the deployment and should be made clear for each use case.

Camera-based physiological measurements are estimates. They are not a diagnosis or a substitute for professional medical advice.
