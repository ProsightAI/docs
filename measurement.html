Measurement

Measurement & Integrity

ProsightAI is designed to make clear what was measured, how strong the evidence is, and when a value should not be shown.

01

Contactless Measurement

ProsightAI uses standard video to estimate heart rate, heart rate variability, and respiratory rate without requiring a wearable sensor.

02

Quality & Confidence

Measurements include quality and confidence information so weak evidence can be identified rather than presented with false certainty.

03

Withholding & Labeling

If evidence is insufficient, a value may be withheld. Demo or simulated values are labeled and are not presented as measured data.

A withheld value with a reason is more truthful than a plausible-looking guess.
